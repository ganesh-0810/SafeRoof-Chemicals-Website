Coating & Protection Solutions

Advanced protective coatings for floors and walls, providing durable resistance against water, chemicals, grease, UV exposure, algae and fungal growth.

Our coating and protection solutions are suitable for industrial floors, walls, concrete structures, commercial buildings, factories, warehouses and other areas requiring reliable surface protection.

The range includes waterproof coatings, protective coatings and epoxy systems designed to improve surface durability, reduce moisture penetration and protect structures from demanding environmental conditions.

Acrylic-Based Waterproof Coating

Flexible acrylic-based protective coatings suitable for walls, roofs and concrete surfaces, providing resistance against water penetration and weather exposure.

Polymer-Based Waterproof Coating

Polymer-based waterproofing and protective coatings designed to provide flexible, durable protection against moisture, water seepage and environmental exposure.

PU-Based Protective Coating

PU-based protective coating solutions designed for specialised surface protection and improved resistance in demanding environments.

Epoxy Coatings

High-performance epoxy coatings providing a strong, seamless and durable protective surface with excellent resistance to chemicals, water, grease, abrasion and heavy usage.

These coating systems can be used for new construction, maintenance and renovation projects where long-lasting surface protection, waterproofing and resistance to environmental exposure are required.
