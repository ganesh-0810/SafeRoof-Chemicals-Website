Retardant, Actuators & Plasticizer

Better concrete performance, workability and controlled setting.

Application focus

Concrete admixture solutions designed to improve workability, control setting time, enhance strength and provide better performance during construction.

Retarders help control the setting time of concrete, especially during hot weather, long transportation periods and large-scale concreting operations.

Plasticizers and high-performance admixtures improve the workability of concrete while helping reduce water requirements and achieve a denser, stronger and more durable concrete mix.

Suitable for RCC structures, slabs, columns, beams, foundations, ready-mix concrete, large pours and other demanding construction applications.
